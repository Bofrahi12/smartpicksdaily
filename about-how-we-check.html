How we check every pick

Smart Picks Daily · Our Method · For adults 18+ in the US

“We Check It First” is a promise, not a slogan. Before anything reaches this site — an app, a survey panel, a cashback flow, an offer — we walk it ourselves, end to end, the way a reader would. If the flow does not match the promise, we say so, and we do not recommend it. This page explains exactly what we check, what we never do, and the real lesson that made the method strict.

1. What we check, step by step

First, we sign up the way a reader would, with honest details and no shortcuts. Then we read the terms — eligibility, payout rules, minimum balances, expiry clocks, data use — before we judge anything. Then we walk the flow end to end: the install or sign-up, the first actions, the points or rewards as they appear, and the payout step itself, including how long it takes and what it asks for. For apps we also check the listing: developer identity, the Data Safety section, recent reviews, and whether the permissions it requests match what it actually does.

2. What we never do

We never publish a claim we have not verified ourselves. We never describe earnings as guaranteed — rewards are modest, a few dollars a week at most for casual use, and nothing is guaranteed. We never hide terms or skip the payout rules to make an offer look better. We never recommend something we have not walked, and we never let sponsorship change whether a pick passes our checks: sponsored links are labelled #ad, and a pick that fails stays failed. If an offer changes after we checked it, we update what we wrote — providers change terms, rates and availability without notice, and a check is only ever a snapshot of what we found that day.

3. A real lesson: the offer we blacklisted

The method became strict because of one walk. We once followed an offer labelled as a survey: the dashboard name and description said survey, and the numbers looked ordinary. The live landing told a different story — it was a phone-billing flow, and the page redirected onward to adult and casino pages. The label lied; the flow told the truth. We blacklisted that offer and never promoted it, and we stopped trusting dashboard labels entirely. Since then, every offer gets a live walk of its landing before it can be recommended, whatever its name or numbers say. That is the single most important rule on this site: labels lie, walk the flow.

4. Our verified install flow

For apps, the check has one more step: the install itself. Our verified flow is simple — pick one app that fits your phone, install it on Android, and open it once so it counts. That is the whole flow, and it is the only one we describe, because it is the one we walked. If an app asks for anything beyond that — a payment before payout, access to contacts or messages it does not need, or a download from outside the official store — that is a warning sign, and we say so. The 60-second safety check and the 10-second check turn this method into two routines any reader can run in a minute.

5. How to use this site

Start with the question you are asking today. Our guides are grouped into three collections: rewards guides for surveys, points and payouts; savings guides for groceries and everyday spending; and apps guides for safety, permissions and payouts. Each guide ends with the checks we run ourselves, so you can repeat them on any app or offer — including ones we have never written about. And if you want the short version, our daily picks gather what passed that day on one page. Whatever you try, read the terms first: nothing is guaranteed, and the terms are where the truth lives.

Key takeaways

  • We sign up, read the terms and walk the flow end to end before we recommend anything.
  • Labels lie — a live walk of the landing is the only check that counts, and one failed walk blacklists an offer for good.
  • Earnings are modest and never guaranteed; sponsorship never changes whether a pick passes.
