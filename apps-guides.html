Before you install anything, run the 10-second check and the 60-second safety check together: developer identity, Data Safety, recent reviews, permissions against purpose, and Play Protect. After installing, run the permissions audit — location, camera, microphone and contacts are the ones to tighten first, and unused apps should lose access they never needed. If the app pays, read how to tell the legit ones from the scams before you spend hours on it: check the withdrawal minimum, the payout method and how long payouts take, and expect modest amounts, never guaranteed. Quick questions

What is the fastest safety check before installing?

The 60-second check: confirm the developer, read the Data Safety section, scan recent reviews, compare requested permissions with what the app actually does, and keep Play Protect on.

Which permission is most abused?

Location, especially “all the time” access. Navigation and ride-sharing apps need it while in use; games, news and shopping apps usually do not. Set it to “while using the app” at most, and deny it where it makes no sense.

Do I need to delete apps I never use?

Not always — but unused apps keep the access you once gave them. Review them in Permission Manager, revoke what they do not need, and turn on “pause app activity if unused” where your phone offers it.
