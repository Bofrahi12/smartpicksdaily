Frequently asked questions

What is Smart Picks Daily?

Smart Picks Daily is a US-focused guide site covering survey sites, rewards apps, cashback and everyday money-saving methods. We walk through each flow ourselves before we write about it.

What does “We check it first” mean?

Before we recommend an app or site, we sign up, read the terms, check the payout rules and walk the flow end to end. If the landing page does not match what was promised, we say so — and we do not recommend it.

Are your links sponsored?

Some links on this site are sponsored affiliate links and are labelled #ad. If you use one, we may earn a commission from the provider. Sponsorship never changes whether a pick passes our checks.

Can surveys or rewards apps guarantee earnings?

No. Nothing is guaranteed. Availability, rewards and payouts vary by provider, country and device, and every offer has its own terms. Always read the terms before you start.

Who is Smart Picks Daily for?

Adults (18+) in the United States who want honest, step-by-step explanations of how survey sites, rewards apps and cashback programs actually work.
